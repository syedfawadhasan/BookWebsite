About the book

Twelve values, one week at a time

Each chapter takes a single Islamic value and follows it through three movements: a short story from everyday Muslim life, what psychology and behavioural science say about it, and a practice small enough to keep for a week.

It is written for readers who want depth without density — something you can read on a commute and still act on the same evening.
