About the author

Leena

Follow @leena.author for first looks

Her work is rooted in one question: what happens when the wisdom of faith meets what we know about the human mind and behaviour?

Leena is a certified life coach, author, and technology professional. With a background in computer engineering, she spent more than thirteen years in software engineering roles at global technology companies. Her writing brings together practical thinking, Islamic values, and a thoughtful approach to personal growth.

This background shapes a structured, practical approach grounded in faith and purpose.

As a coach, Leena helps people clarify what matters to them and bring those values into their choices, relationships, and responsibilities.

Tomorrow Takes Practice grew from this work and her study of Islamic guidance for daily life.
