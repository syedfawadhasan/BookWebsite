About the author

Leena

Her work is rooted in one question: what happens when the wisdom of faith meets what we know about the human mind and behaviour?

Leena is a certified life coach, author, and technology professional who brings together psychology, faith, and practical life experience to help people create lives that feel more meaningful and aligned.

With a background in software engineering and more than 13 years in technology, Leena has spent her career solving complex problems, adapting to change, and navigating the many roles and demands of life. As a certified life coach, she brings that same analytical, problem solving mindset to her work, combining logic with empathy, reflection, and practical strategies.

Her work is rooted in a deeper question: What happens when the wisdom of faith meets what we know about the human mind and behavior?

In her book, Leena explores the connection between Islamic values and psychology, showing how timeless principles of faith can offer practical tools for navigating stress, purpose, relationships, personal growth, and the challenges of everyday life. Rather than treating faith and psychology as separate worlds, she explores how the two can complement each other to foster greater self awareness, resilience, and alignment.

Leena is also trained in Ikigai, the exploration of purpose and meaning, as well as mindfulness coaching, giving her a unique blend of structured thinking and soulful reflection.

Her approach is simple: no fluff, no judgment, just meaningful conversations, practical tools, and a path toward a life that feels more intentional, grounded, and fulfilling.

Through her writing and coaching, Leena invites you to pause, reflect, reconnect with what matters, and turn the question "What now?" into a confident "Let's go."
