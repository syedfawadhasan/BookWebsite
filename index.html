About the Book

A practical path to emotional clarity, spiritual strength, and peace.

This is a short, practical book on emotional well-being from an Islamic perspective. Built around twelve core Islamic values — tafakkur (reflection), muraqabah (awareness of Allah), sabr (patience), tawakkul (trust in Allah), and eight others — it brings these ideas into ordinary Muslim life through engaging, fictional stories.

The narratives explore conversations, decisions, relationships, work, family life, mistakes, pressure, and healing — the everyday moments where character is actually formed. Rather than staying at the level of the lecture, the book asks: what does this value look like when you're the one living it?

Before writing began, a focus group of ten readers — including working professionals, physicians, and community members — reviewed the manuscript and shaped its final form.
